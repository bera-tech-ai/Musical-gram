Frequently asked questions

How do I pay?

Select a subscription, enter a valid Kenyan M-Pesa number, and submit the payment request. Verify the amount before approving any M-Pesa prompt.

What happens after payment?

The page checks the transaction status through your server. When the server confirms success, you can continue to WhatsApp for activation assistance.

What if the payment prompt does not arrive?

Check that your phone number is correct, your phone can receive M-Pesa prompts, and your network is working. You can try again if no transaction was initiated.

Are subscriptions activated automatically?

Automatic activation requires a separate integration. This page directs successful customers to WhatsApp for assistance; payment confirmation alone does not activate a third-party subscription.
